On this page

1. Executive Summary

LokalBot is a local-first macOS app, with a Rust Windows and Linux port in alpha, that records meetings without a bot, keeps screen and activity memory, and answers questions over them with local models. Most of the code is capture, transcription, search, autocomplete and the agent surfaces. The agent memory in it is small: Personal Context, an encrypted list of at most 300 claims about the person, and the work memory the nightly review keeps of projects and goals. Their services are about 2% of the Swift and 1% of the Rust. This report is scoped to them; the meeting library and screen memory appear only as the evidence they are learned from.

What is notable is that a learned claim depends on its source. The overnight pass reads only the person's own lines and keeps an item only if it is grounded in the line it cites. A claim learned from a meeting is retracted when that meeting is deleted, or when a correction removes the quoted line. Forgetting or dismissing a learned claim leaves a hash of its text that the next night's writer checks.

What is weak is the routing. For a meeting line the model's own kind label decides whether an item waits for review: a fact or goal is saved and used at once. Editing a claim leaves the old value learnable, a restated fact sits beside the one it contradicts, and tombstones lapse after 180 days.

Licence. The AGPL-3.0 network clause reaches the whole app, not only the code it covers. Cotabby-derived autocomplete parts "remain under the GNU Affero General Public License version 3", and, as GPL-3.0 section 13 provides, "AGPL-3.0's section 13 requirements on interaction through a network apply to the combination as such" (THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md:44-56). Anyone who offers a modified LokalBot to users over a network owes them the complete source, whichever part was changed. The rest of the tree is GPL-3.0-only in LICENSE and LinuxWindows/Cargo.toml:9, and the memory code is GPL-3.0. Models are downloaded, not bundled. The notices say the LFM licence carries a commercial-use revenue threshold (THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md:40-42), and LFM2.5 is the Lightweight preset's autocomplete model.

2. Mental Model

A claim is one sentence about the person, of one of four kinds: fact, preference, goal or principle (LokalBot/Services/PersonalModel/PersonalClaim.swift:10-78). Each claim carries an origin, a status and an authority. Status is confirmed (in use) or proposed ("waiting for the person's review; never used"). Authority is stated, learned, or confirmed for a learned claim the person kept (:46-60). A learned claim also carries evidence, the meeting or day it came from, and quote, the person's own line.

There are two ways in. The person types Remember … in Ask, which a parser recognizes in English and Serbian, or adds a claim in Settings. Either way it is confirmed and stated, with no evidence (:521-557, :315-338). The second way is the overnight pass. It reads the day's own-speech meeting turns and typed lines, asks the Think model for at most three items, and keeps only those grounded in the line they cite (PersonalLearning.swift:551-570). PersonalModel.learn then decides where each lands (PersonalClaim.swift:433-466):

  • a principle is never learned;
  • a duplicate, a near-duplicate of a current claim, or a value matching a tombstone is skipped;
  • a preference, or any item flagged for review, becomes proposed;
  • anything else, a fact or goal from a meeting line, is confirmed with authority learned.

A claim stops being believed in four ways. Forget removes it, and for a learned claim writes a tombstone keyed on a SHA-256 of its normalized text (:369-374). Dismiss does the same to a suggestion (:489-491). Retraction removes a learned claim, with no tombstone, when its source changed and the source no longer holds the quoted line (:378-380, PersonalModelController.swift:91-111). Expiry drops a suggestion nobody reviewed after 14 days (PersonalClaim.swift:506-510). Saying a learned claim again, or Keep as statement, makes it the person's own. Its evidence is dropped, so deleting the meeting no longer removes it (:326-330, :496-503).

The work memory is a second, separate belief store. The nightly review's model proposes full lists of projects, goals and patterns, and app code merges them (LokalBot/Services/Dreaming/DreamModels.swift:336-430). An entry the model stops mentioning is kept until it ages out, after 30 days for a project and 45 for a goal (:279-280). A goal leaves early only when the day's evidence marks it completed or abandoned. Every entry carries the provenance of what the synthesis could read, and a source deletion removes it, pinned or not (:452-471).

3. Architecture

The app is the system. Personal Context is one file, memory/personal-model.json.enc, sealed with AES-GCM under the per-install chat-key from the Keychain, written atomically with mode 0600 (LokalBot/Services/PersonalModel/PersonalModelStore.swift:4-90). A file that cannot be opened, or one saved by a newer version, makes every edit fail rather than be overwritten (:52-74, PersonalModelController.swift:283-301). PersonalModelController is the single owner. It loads lazily so launch never touches the Keychain for it.

The work memory is memory/memory.json with a rendered memory.md, plaintext at 0600, beside one report per analysed day in dreams/ (LokalBot/Services/Dreaming/DreamStore.swift:32-44, :555-574). An evidence journal, memory/evidence-revisions.json, holds a store revision and the revision at which each day, meeting or activity day was last invalidated. A pending-revocation file at the library root blocks every reader while a retraction is unfinished, and an advisory flock makes the revision check and the commit atomic across the app and the headless runner (:52-84, :499-521).

Agents never read the encrypted file. While the person's Personal context grant is on, the app writes control/personal-context.json, a plaintext copy of the claims marked shared. It rewrites the copy on every change and deletes it on any failure (LokalBot/Services/PersonalModel/PersonalContextAccessManager.swift:54-79). lokalbot-cli personal-context and the MCP tool get_personal_context read only that copy (LokalBot/CLISupport/PersonalContextAccess.swift:293-349).

The Rust app in LinuxWindows/ reimplements the same model, controller, learning pass and projection, and reads the Mac's file format (LinuxWindows/crates/lokalbot-core/src/personal/mod.rs, controller.rs, LinuxWindows/crates/lokalbot-ai/src/personal_learning.rs). An encrypted file opens only on the computer that saved it.

Deployment and ergonomics

Nothing has to run beside the app. Learning needs the Think model, local by default; a remote engine needs explicit approval, and an automatic run stops unless the settings allow automatic Think inference (LokalBot/AppState.swift:3303). Cross-language grounding needs the local EmbeddingGemma embedder. Without semantic search a translated item is dropped rather than saved (:3327-3331). Storing a claim needs no model and no key. The Personal Context file is not readable by hand, by design; the work memory is, and Settings offers per-claim edit, forget and Forget everything (PersonalModelController.swift:257-271).

4. Essential Implementation Paths

  • Remember. ChatViewModel.send matches RememberIntent.parse on the visible text and saves through PersonalModelController.remember with no model call, then appends a receipt message (LokalBot/Services/Chat/ChatViewModel.swift:579-586, :644-681).
  • Validate. validatedText sanitizes, refuses empty text, refuses text over 280 characters rather than cutting it ("a cut sentence can drop a negation"), and refuses text the screen-privacy redactor would scrub as a secret (PersonalClaim.swift:294-302).
  • Learn. AppState.learnAboutPerson runs after each night's review. It first promotes long-running work memory, then gathers the day's lines, skips a day already read with the same fingerprint, and captures the evidence revision before the model call (LokalBot/AppState.swift:3293-3346). PersonalLearning.utterances takes own-microphone turns and skips a meeting whose microphone was shared by voices it cannot tell apart, unless one voice dominates four to one (LokalBot/Services/PersonalModel/PersonalLearning.swift:217-249). It keeps only lines with a first-person word, at most 40 lines and 6,000 characters, of which 15 may be typed (:62-68, :77-126).
  • Ground. proposals drops items citing no real handle or of an unknown kind. isGrounded needs a third of the item's content words in the cited line. A translated item is compared by embedding: below 0.82 it is dropped, and between 0.82 and 0.90 it waits for review unless a word root matches (:379-395, :410-415, :430-484).
  • Route. candidate sets needsReview for every typed line, whatever kind the model chose (:515-526). WorkMemoryPromotion.evaluate suggests a goal or project the review has kept for 14 days and seen in the last 7, always for review, and never one built on unattributed context (LokalBot/Services/PersonalModel/WorkMemoryPromotion.swift:20-49).
  • Retract. A meeting deletion or correction calls DreamStore.invalidateEvidence, which writes the pending intent, advances the revision, re-filters the work memory and removes invalidated reports (DreamStore.swift:199-283). The app then calls retractIfLoaded (AppState.swift:2663). Every read of learned claims re-runs retractInvalidated anyway. It keeps a changed claim whose source still holds its quote and removes the rest (PersonalModelController.swift:91-111, AppState.swift:3351-3372).
  • Use. PersonalContextBuilder.select orders the usable claims and packs them into 2,000 characters, skipping what does not fit (LokalBot/Services/PersonalModel/PersonalContextBuilder.swift:11-37).

5. Memory Data Model

A PersonalClaim has id, kind, text, origin, alwaysUse, sharing, status, authority, evidence, quote, createdAt, updatedAt and lastUsedAt (PersonalClaim.swift:62-78). origin says where the words came from: said in Ask, added in Settings, learned from a meeting, typed to a coding agent, asked in Ask, dictated, or promoted from work memory (:13-36). evidence is a DreamEvidenceProvenance: the meeting, screen day or digest sources plus the store revision captured before they were read (DreamModels.swift:81-121).

The model file also holds tombstones (hash and date), workSightings (when each work goal or project was first seen) and learnedDays (a day key and a fingerprint of the lines read) (PersonalClaim.swift:217-250). Limits are 300 confirmed claims, 20 open suggestions, 280 characters, and lifetimes of 14 days for suggestions, 180 for tombstones and 60 for learned days (:187-244). A full model refuses new claims and never evicts one (:334).

There is no scope key. It is one person's library, and the only partitions are sharing (lokalBotOnly or agents) and the authority filter scoped questions apply. There is no validity time: createdAt and updatedAt are record times, and a goal's horizon in the work memory is free text, "never a normalized date" (DreamModels.swift:297-299).

The design document describes fields the code does not carry. Its claim shape has supersedes, reviewAfter, sensitive and domains, and its tombstone is kind plus text hash (Docs/designs/personal-world-model-rfc-2026-10-14.md:112-137). The code keys tombstones on text alone. Sensitive topics are excluded by the learning prompt, not by a flag (PersonalLearning.swift:307).

6. Retrieval Mechanics

There is no search over claims. PersonalContextSelection.ordered puts Always use claims first, then every principle and preference, then facts and goals sharing content words with the question and the person's last two messages, then the rest, newest first within each step (LokalBot/CLISupport/PersonalContextAccess.swift:29-59). Content words are folded, at least three letters long, and not stopwords (:80-90). The CLI and MCP tool use the same ordering over the shared copy, so an agent and Ask rank alike.

Every block is labelled. A learned claim is rendered as learned with its date. The header says the items "are not evidence", and the footer tells the model to prefer the newer of two conflicting items and to say so (PersonalContextBuilder.swift:39-50). The work memory is appended whole, up to 6,000 characters, and only to unscoped questions (LokalBot/Services/Chat/ChatAgent.swift:135-138, ChatViewModel.swift:1100-1102).

Two failure modes follow from the ordering. Every preference goes in before any matching fact, so a large preference list crowds facts out of the budget. A two-letter token such as CI is never a content word, a miss the GBrain comparison recorded (Benchmarks/memory/GBrain/2026-11-17/REPORT.md:188-190).

7. Write Mechanics

A Remember is synchronous and immediate: one sealed write, no model call. A learned claim waits for the nightly review. It is retrievable the next morning, or, as a suggestion, only after the person keeps it. The pass makes one model call per day read, at temperature 0 with at most 1,024 output tokens. It does not run again for a day whose own lines are unchanged (PersonalLearning.swift:560-570, AppState.swift:3317-3321). Dictated text is kept only until that pass reads it (AppState.swift:3334-3337).

The writer defends the store in five places:

  • grounding against the cited line;
  • an exact-key and near-duplicate check against current claims, at 70% of the shorter item's content words (PersonalClaim.swift:441-447, :470-476);
  • the tombstone check;
  • the review routing;
  • the known list sent to the model, which includes open suggestions so the model does not repeat them (AppState.swift:3326).

Nothing detects a contradiction. I live in Novi Sad, stated after I live in Belgrade, is a second claim, and both reach the prompt with their dates.

The tombstone is narrower than forgetting:

  • Only forget and dismiss write one. update replaces the text and writes none, so a value the person corrected by editing can be learned again from a later day's line (PersonalClaim.swift:340-365).
  • A stated claim leaves none. That includes a learned claim the person kept as a statement and later forgot (:369-374).
  • A source retraction leaves none, by design. The value may be learned again from evidence that still exists (:376-380).
  • Forget everything deletes the tombstones with the file (PersonalModelStore.swift:92-98).

The work memory is rewritten by every review: the model sees the current memory and returns full lists, and the merge caps them at 12 projects, 10 goals and 12 patterns (DreamModels.swift:271-280). Settings can pin an entry or clear all of it. A single entry cannot be deleted. Only clearing, a source deletion or age-out removes it (DreamStore.swift:350-377, :418-434).

On the read path, Personal Context costs at most 2,000 characters and work memory at most 6,000 per Ask turn. Both sit in the system prompt and change with the question, so a cached system-prompt prefix does not survive a new question.

8. Agent Integration

LokalBot's own Ask agent receives Personal Context automatically and has no tool that writes it. External agents read through lokalbot-cli or its stdio MCP server, which the README describes as read-only. Personal context needs its own grant on top of the general one, and returns only shared claims (LokalBot/CLISupport/PersonalContextAccess.swift:315-318). The bundled Claude Code plugin and the Agent Mode skill tell the agent to call personal-context "before advising on a personal decision". They also tell it never to create or edit files in LokalBot's control folder (LokalBot/Resources/pi/lokalbot-cli-skill/SKILL.md:23, :85). Agent Mode runs a general shell, so that last rule is a request to the model.

What agents type is memory input. With Read coding agent sessions on, the person's requests to Claude Code and Codex become typed lines for the overnight pass. Anything learned from them waits for review, and deleting the saved sessions retracts it (PersonalLearning.swift:532-545). The app also mirrors the notes Claude Code and Codex keep for themselves into a rebuildable SQLite index for search_agent_notes (LokalBot/Services/AgentMemory/AgentMemoryStore.swift:9-14). That index is a copy of other agents' memory, not LokalBot's.

9. Reliability, Safety, and Trust

What admits a learned claim

The overnight model cannot write status. It returns line, kind and text under a JSON schema, and app code sets status, authority, evidence and quote (PersonalLearning.swift:338-395, PersonalClaim.swift:456-461). It can choose kind, and for a meeting line that choice decides whether the item waits: a preference labelled a fact is saved and used at once. Every typed, dictated and work-memory item waits, whatever the kind. Only the person's microphone turns are read, so another speaker in a meeting cannot plant a claim. "My microphone is me" is assumed for older transcripts with no attribution (PersonalLearning.swift:251-257).

What a deletion reaches

Source deletion is the strongest part. The revision is advanced and the intent written before the memory journal, and a failure blocks every reader in every process until a retry succeeds (DreamStore.swift:199-283). A generation that started before a deletion cannot commit its output (:398-413). Learned claims are withheld while a revocation is pending, not served stale (PersonalModelController.swift:65-73). Forgetting a claim removes the record from the sealed file. A plaintext copy of every shared claim sits in control/ while the grant is on.

Marks

  • tombstone — awarded on the value-hash ledger the overnight writer checks before saving. It holds no text. It expires, an edit does not write one, and a reworded value passes it (section 7).
  • trust_state — awarded on status. proposed claims are in no prompt and no agent projection. authority additionally filters scoped questions, and pending revocation withholds evidence-backed claims.
  • human_review — awarded on the suggestion queue. Keep and Dismiss exist only in Settings on both platforms, and no CLI, MCP or Agent Mode surface reaches the personal model. The gap is that the model's kind decides whether a meeting-derived item enters the queue.
  • negative_eval — awarded; see section 10.
  • scope_enforced — withheld. There is no user, project or tenant key. sharing is applied when the app writes the agent copy, a separate file, so the agent boundary is a physical partition.
  • bitemporal — withheld. Claims carry record times only.
  • audit_log — withheld. No mutation record exists for claims or work memory. The evidence journal holds revision numbers, not changes.

I ran none of it.

negative_eval rests on populated read-path cases:

  • LokalBotTests/Services/PersonalModel/PersonalLearningTests.swift:357-378 learns two claims from two meetings beside a stated one, invalidates one meeting, and asserts the usable claims are exactly the other two. A reopened controller must hold two, so the retraction was saved and not just filtered.
  • :380-398 withholds the learned claim while a revocation is pending, keeps it in the file, and serves it again afterwards.
  • LinuxWindows/crates/lokalbot-core/src/personal/tests.rs:722-770 marks a proposed preference as shared and asserts the agent projection is empty. It then confirms the claim and asserts the projection holds one, and blocks evidence and asserts the projection is empty again.

Write-side cases cover the tombstone: a forgotten fact and a dismissed preference are not learned again, and a retraction writes none (PersonalLearningTests.swift:268-288, tests.rs:350-403). The Dream store has 19 retraction tests, including an empty regeneration that must not bring back deleted memory (LokalBotTests/Services/Dreaming/DreamMemoryRetractionTests.swift:311-327). No test asserts that an edited value stays out of a later night's learning, and section 7 shows it does not.

Two committed benchmarks touch this memory. No paper is associated with the project.

  • Benchmarks/memory/GBrain/2026-11-17/ compares Ask's meeting retrieval and Personal Context corrections with GBrain on QMSum. The headline hybrid result, R@1 0.280 against 0.239, does not recompute from the tree: the Mac run's per-question files "stayed on the Mac" (REPORT.md:247-249). The keyword run's raw files are committed. From results-cloud/raw.tar.gz, with my own script, I recomputed R@1 0.193 against 0.253, R@4 0.335 against 0.412 and MRR 0.274 against 0.325 over 1,576 questions, as published. The Personal Context counts also recompute: current 39/40 after an edit; stale 40/40 after a restatement, current first in 28; 0/40 after a forget. The forget case removes stated facts and never re-runs learning, so it does not exercise the tombstone.
  • Benchmarks/think/MemoryRetention/2026-10-23/ scores four local models on the learning prompt over 120 authored cases. Every non-comment line of its PersonalLearning.swift.snapshot matches the pinned file. The report's English table matches scores.json: Qwen3.5 9B recalled 37/52 eligible items with 5 unwanted proposals and was correct on 40/44 empty cases, and Hy-Memory 30B recalled 52/52 with 76 unwanted and 0/44 empty. I did not re-score the 480 raw completions. The report states that the assistant authored the cases and reviewed outputs with model identity visible.

11. For Your Own Build

Steal

  • Tie learned memory to its evidence, and keep what the person said. Record the source and a store revision on each learned item, and drop it when the source is deleted or the quoted line corrected. A claim the person states or keeps outlives the source.
  • Withhold while a revocation is unfinished. A pending-intent file that blocks every reader until cleanup succeeds turns a half-finished deletion into a refusal rather than a stale answer.
  • Hash the rejected value. A SHA-256 of the normalized text stops re-learning and stores nothing the person asked to forget.
  • Route by source, not by the model's confidence. Everything learned from typing or dictation waits for review regardless of what the model called it.
  • Refuse over-long text instead of truncating it, because a cut sentence can drop the negation.

Avoid

  • Letting the extractor's label pick the gate. If one kind is reviewed and another is not, the model choosing the kind chooses the review.
  • Treating an edit as anything but a rejection of the old value. Write the tombstone on replacement too, or the corrected value returns the next time it is said.
  • An expiring tombstone without saying so to the person. "Forgotten" that lasts 180 days is a different promise.

Fit

This suits a single-user desktop assistant that learns from a person's own meetings and typing, where privacy and the right to delete matter more than recall. Its design argues that a small, reviewed, source-linked profile is worth more than a large inferred one. It has no notion of a team, a second user or a shared library, and nothing in it tracks when a fact was true. A builder who needs contradiction handling, history, or memory shared across people should take the retraction machinery and build the rest. Compare Screenpipe, a desktop recorder in the same product class that remembers only what someone writes down.

12. Open Questions

  • Whether meeting-derived facts and goals are meant to bypass review when the model's kind is wrong, given that the memory-retention benchmark scores kind errors separately.
  • Whether update should tombstone the replaced value, as the GBrain report's third recommendation implies for restatements.
  • What the Mac hybrid run's per-question files show; they are not committed.
  • How often a person's microphone carries another voice that diarization does not split, which the four-to-one dominance rule would then accept.
