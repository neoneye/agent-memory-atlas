On this page

1. Executive Summary

Sonder Runtime is a self-modifying terminal agent whose memory credits the outcome of each interaction back to the lessons retrieved for it, and quarantines a lesson only when its run of losses is improbable for its own retrieval-frequency band and it is individually answerable for them. The weak side is the read surface around that loop: an agent tool searches every project's facts and interactions, and machine-graded outcomes drive the quarantine at full weight.

Most of the runtime is out of this atlas's scope: a code-rewriting loop (selfmod.py), a training pipeline, an NPU broker, multi-agent lanes. What is in scope is a stdlib-only SQLite adapter (sonder_runtime/adapters/memory_store.py) holding interactions, the outcomes credited to them, the lessons distilled from them, project facts and preferences, plus a Markdown store of owner-reviewed playbooks beside it.

The quarantine is careful in the places this idea is usually done badly:

  • Blame is split across co-retrieved lessons. A failing task writes its reward onto every lesson retrieved for it, so each loss row counts as 1/cohort and the gate needs 2.0 attributable losses (retriever.py:54, :319). test_one_failure_cluster_no_longer_quarantines_its_whole_cohort pins it.
  • A loss run is judged against the lesson's own base rate. Seven frequency bands carry measured loss rates from 50.62% down to 1.24%, and the run must be improbable at p ≤ 0.01 for the lesson's band (retriever.py:73-89). The band test computes an explicit p=0.006.
  • Quarantine has a route back. After 24 hours a quarantined lesson is admitted on a deterministic one-in-twenty sample of tasks, and a win lifts the quarantine on evidence rather than on the seven-day timer (retriever.py:99-100, :494).

The whole apparatus rests on who judged the outcome. outcomes.source is NOT NULL with deliberately no default and a closed vocabulary — caller, machine, attributed, self_curriculum, unknown (memory_store.py:73). The quarantine excludes heuristically attributed evidence and counts machine evidence fully, by design: a test is named test_directly_graded_machine_evidence_still_drives_the_gate. The ranking tiebreak prefers the caller's population wherever a caller has judged.

Two further mechanisms sit beside the lessons. Playbooks are agent-written notes that wait as proposed until the owner approves them at the CLI or REPL, and only approved entries reach the prompt. Facts carry an optional validity interval the caller asserts, separate from the write time, and a successor closes its predecessor's interval.

The rejected-value story is half-closed. Near-duplicate pruning writes a content-hashed tombstone that distillation refuses to re-derive, but quarantine suppresses a lesson without one, so a lesson quarantined as harmful and then re-distilled from a fresh interaction can return.

2. Mental Model

A memory is a lesson, and its life is governed by the outcomes of the interactions that retrieve it.

interaction happens   -> interactions row (task, retrieved_ctx, response, project, project_explicit)
outcome lands         -> outcomes row (signal, reward, source)  -- source NOT NULL, closed vocabulary
distillation claims   -> lesson_distillations: claimed -> stored | no_lesson | cancelled
                         refusing not_concrete, exact_duplicate, semantic_duplicate, rejected_value
lesson stored         -> lessons row + embedding + lessons_fts
lesson retrieved      -> lesson_usage row links (lesson, interaction)
outcome credited      -> lesson_usage.reward and outcome_source filled in
losses accumulate     -> quarantine when floor, attribution and band test all agree
quarantine            -> excluded before ranking; after 24 h sampled probation; a win exits
pruned as duplicate   -> row deleted, lesson_tombstones row keyed on the text hash

Lessons are the procedural layer, and four other durable kinds sit beside them.

  • Facts are asserted, not earned: sonder_remember_fact stores a project fact that is injected into every call for that project. A fact may carry valid_from and valid_until, and supersedes=<id> closes the earlier fact's interval as the new one is stored. A fact outside its interval is kept and not recalled.
  • Preferences carry confidence, evidence_count, enabled and a revision under UNIQUE(scope, key); a disabled preference is filtered out of reads.
  • Playbook entries are owner-curated notes in Markdown. The agent proposes one with playbook_note; it stays proposed until the owner approves or rejects it; only approved entries enter the system-prompt index or a topic read.
  • Session summaries condense a conversation per project.

How a lesson stops being believed has three paths, and they differ in what they remember:

  • Quarantine — excluded from retrieval because the outcomes credited to it went bad, statistically and answerably. Reversible by a probation win. The row is kept and no rejected value is recorded.
  • Near-duplicate pruning — deleted because another lesson says the same thing (cosine ≥ 0.93), keeping one representative and writing a lesson_tombstones row that distillation consults by hash and by embedding.
  • Age-decay ranking — demoted over a 30-day default half-life blended with usage evidence; not exclusion.

A fact stops being believed when its interval closes, either by the valid_until it was written with or by a successor; it can also be deleted by exact id and project. A playbook entry stops being believed when the owner rejects or supersedes it.

3. Architecture

One local SQLite database is the spine. sonder_runtime/adapters/memory_store.py defines interactions, outcomes, lessons with lessons_fts, lesson_tombstones, lesson_usage, lesson_distillations, facts, preferences, refinement_history, sessions, session_project_summaries, tasks with task_events and task_deps, an outbox_events table, and the authoritative-fact journal tables. The adapter needs no third-party package: it imports the standard library (sqlite3, array, hashlib, math among others) and sibling modules of sonder_runtime. Embeddings are BLOBs stamped with embedding_model, embedding_revision and embedding_dim, and retrieval skips a stored vector from a different space rather than scoring it zero.

The memory logic sits in small modules wired to the adapter:

  • retriever.py (670 lines) — hybrid lesson retrieval, MMR selection and the quarantine decision (lesson_quarantine, band_loss_rate, attributable_losses, _usage_boost).
  • reflection.py (520) — distillation and the tombstone refusal; its prompts load from editable files under prompts/ through prompt_store.
  • lesson_decay.py (342) — pure age-decay and usage ranking, and detect_contradictions, which memory_quality.contradiction_findings wires to the store as a report-only finding.
  • lesson_pruner.py (327) — embedding-cluster near-duplicate removal that tombstones each loser after revalidating both vectors under a write lock.
  • memory_quality.py (707) — a read-only auditor for vague lessons, duplicates, contradictions and playbook quality.
  • sonder_runtime/adapters/playbook_store.py (405) with playbook_review.py, domain/memory/playbooks.py and application/memory/playbook_context.py — the playbook store, its approval rules and its prompt selection.
  • sonder_runtime/domain/memory/fact_validity.py — the validity interval and the predicate every current-fact read uses.
  • sonder_runtime/adapters/persistence/sqlite/authoritative_memory.py and memory_replication.py — an opt-in authoritative fact source with an ordered journal.

The root memory_store.py is a compatibility alias that replaces itself in sys.modules with the adapter. sonder_runtime/application/memory/strategy_memory.py indexes content-free strategy history from sealed runtime attempts into the same database; it is peripheral to the lesson loop and not read further here.

Deployment and ergonomics

  • One SQLite file, no services. The adapter needs nothing beyond the standard library; embeddings come from a local model through embeddings.py.
  • The store is inspectable with any SQLite client, and store_integrity.py, sonder_doctor.py and memory_quality.py are shipped audit tools; connect_read_only opens a mode=ro URI so an inspector cannot migrate it.
  • Playbooks are plain Markdown under the state home, parsed strictly: a block with a forged nested entry or an unknown status is skipped.
  • Concurrency is handled for a local store: claim tokens with owner pid and identity on session turns and distillation, and abandoned-claim recovery guarded by process-liveness checks.
  • Hosted accounts do not use this memory. An account-backed HTTP request passes augment=False, so it neither injects nor trains the lesson store, and the global memory tools are refused at the HTTP boundary (sonder_runtime/interfaces/http/serve.py:1593, :7647).

The screen of this checkout found no auto-run surface, two build-time execution points (conftest.py and tests/conftest.py), two unpinned requirement files (requirements-dev.txt, requirements-train.txt) and no dependency surface inside the cooldown. The licence is Apache-2.0 with no rider. Nothing was installed or run; the code was read and cross-checked against its committed tests.

4. Essential Implementation Paths

  • Write / distill — reflection.maybe_add_lesson and the lesson_distillations state machine: claim an interaction, extract a lesson through reflection_distill, refuse not_concrete (reflection.py:328), exact_duplicate (:428), a tombstoned text (:430) and a tombstoned embedding (:446, via is_tombstoned_duplicate at :146).
  • Credit — memory_store.record_outcome_row (memory_store.py:965) requires a source; lesson_usage rows for the interaction receive the reward and outcome_source. lesson_usage_history (:2347) drops attributed rows from quarantine evidence.
  • Retrieve lessons — retriever.retrieve_with_ids (retriever.py:576): compute quarantined, over-fetch FTS hits by that many, rank compatible vectors with exclude_ids=quarantined (:612), fuse by RRF with a bounded _usage_boost, keep candidates clearing 0.62 (0.70 without two content-word anchors), then memory_rerank.mmr_rerank at lambda 0.5.
  • Quarantine — retriever.lesson_quarantine (:391) with QUARANTINE_MIN_LOSSES = 5, QUARANTINE_REPEAT_TASK_MIN_LOSSES = 6, QUARANTINE_MIN_DISTINCT_TASKS = 2, QUARANTINE_MAX_AVG_REWARD = -0.5, QUARANTINE_MIN_ATTRIBUTABLE_LOSSES = 2.0, QUARANTINE_BAND_ALPHA = 0.01, probation after 24 hours at one in twenty, cooldown seven days.
  • Assemble the prompt — server.py:4191 reads facts_for_project(conn, project), newest first; recall reads good_interaction_candidate_page with i.project=? AND i.project_explicit=1 (memory_store.py:3389-3402); the approved playbook index enters the system prompt (server.py:2446).
  • Facts — sonder_remember_fact (server.py:14438) parses validity through fact_write_inputs, refuses a duplicate within the project (find_duplicate_fact_in, memory_store.py:3586) and calls MemoryRepositoryAdapter.add_fact, which routes to the authoritative source when one is composed and to memory_store.add_fact (:3624) otherwise. sonder_forget_fact deletes by exact id and project after a matching confirm.
  • Playbooks — playbook_note (sonder_runtime/bootstrap/playbooks.py:93) writes a proposed entry and records a pending ledger item; the owner's approve runs approve_pending then PlaybookStore.review (playbook_store.py:291) with the content digest.
  • Prune — lesson_pruner.prune from scripts/nightly_self_improve.py:544 with dry_run=False; apply_plan (lesson_pruner.py:246) revalidates both vectors under BEGIN IMMEDIATE and calls memory_store.tombstone_lesson (memory_store.py:2271).
  • Search — memory_search (server.py:16651), an MCP tool and an agent tool, reads lessons by FTS and facts, preferences, sessions and interactions by LIKE over the whole database.

5. Memory Data Model

The schema is where the epistemic care shows, and several columns carry a comment naming the failure they prevent.

outcomes.source is the sharpest: NOT NULL with no default, a closed CHECK vocabulary, and a comment stating why —

a default is how the original defect would come back, because a writer that forgot would keep filing rows under a meaning it never chose.

The system cannot record an outcome without saying who judged it. lesson_usage.outcome_source is nullable because a usage row exists before any outcome credits it, and the evidence query keeps NULL rows on purpose so the gate does not lose its pre-column history.

lesson_tombstones is the rejected-value record: primary key the normalized text's SHA-256, carrying embedding and vector provenance, and by an explicit comment never the text. reason admits only near_duplicate_pruned. Deleting a source interaction purges the tombstones derived from it (memory_store.py:733). It is kept distinct from privacy deletion: delete_lesson erases, tombstone_lesson rejects a value and remembers, content-free, that it did.

facts gained valid_from, valid_to and superseded_by beside ts, as fixed-width UTC text so SQL compares them as strings. The columns are additive and unbackfilled, so a legacy fact has no interval and stays current. Valid time comes from the caller and must carry a timezone; ts is the write clock. Closing a predecessor overwrites its valid_to and sets superseded_by, and the UPDATE re-checks superseded_by IS NULL, so a fact gains at most one successor (memory_store.py:3674-3703). When the correction was recorded is recoverable only from the successor's ts.

preferences carries scope TEXT DEFAULT 'global', confidence, evidence_count, enabled and revision. Production writes go through upsert_preference (memory_store.py:3771), which overwrites the text and bumps revision, and set_preference_enabled (:3974). refinement_history is append-only by trigger and has a writer, refinement_transactions.apply_preference_refinement, with no caller outside its tests; the repository's own landscape note calls it "fully built with no production route", and the grep agrees.

Playbook entries are Markdown blocks whose metadata lines carry id, date, category, status, tainted and a JSON provenance, followed by Body, Evidence and Triggers. The parser drops a block whose status is outside the four values or that nests a second entry header.

Scoping has three different keys. Interactions, facts and sessions carry project, with project_explicit on interactions so a migrated row with a plausible project string is not recalled into it. Preferences carry scope. Tasks carry account_scope, an opaque digest derived from the authenticated HTTP principal. Lessons and playbooks carry none: procedural knowledge is meant to cross projects.

The project filter is applied on prompt assembly and not on search. facts_for_project, the interaction recall page and find_duplicate_fact_in take one project, and the last says why in its docstring: "scoping is a privacy boundary, not a convenience filter." memory_search (server.py:16651) takes a query and a limit and reads facts, preferences, sessions and interactions with no project or scope predicate. It is on REPOSITORY_READ_ONLY_TOOLS (server.py:17285) and _PROJECT_BOUND_AGENT_TOOLS (:19501), and _agent_dispatch routes it (:18516). An agent working in one project can therefore read another project's facts and transcripts in one call, and scope_enforced is not awarded. The hosted HTTP surface refuses this tool to accounts, on the stated ground that direct MCP and local-open deployments belong to one operator.

6. Retrieval Mechanics

Lesson retrieval is hybrid and outcome-filtered. The task is embedded and scored by cosine against every stored vector in the same embedding space, FTS5 supplies lexical candidates, and the two lists are fused by reciprocal rank with a bounded tiebreak from outcome history. A fused candidate must clear 0.62 cosine, or 0.70 when it shares fewer than two content words with the task; the comment cites a reproduced cross-domain false positive at 0.650211. MMR at lambda 0.5 then picks the final k. Quarantined lessons are removed before any of this: the same set is excluded from the vector ranking and filtered out of the over-fetched FTS hits.

The quarantine decision is evidence-gated, not score-gated: a lesson is dropped for a documented run of bad outcomes it is answerable for, not for low similarity, so retrieval quality and lesson trust are separate axes. The tiebreak (_usage_boost) reads the caller's mean wherever a caller has judged the lesson and the execution mean only otherwise, capped at ±0.01, because a blended mean let eight self-graded passes outrank a caller's rejection.

The base-rate test needs evidence, so a new lesson cannot be quarantined and a rarely retrieved one accumulates evidence slowly. That is the right direction to fail, and it gives a harmful new lesson a grace period proportional to how often it is used.

Fact retrieval is a filter, not a ranking: every current fact of the project is a candidate for the bounded facts block, newest first, round-robin with preference facts. Playbook selection matches topic triggers against the task and reads up to twelve approved entries within a byte budget.

memory_search is the exception to every filter above. It returns FTS lesson hits through get_lesson_text without the quarantine set, so a quarantined lesson reaches the agent through its own tool, and it returns other projects' rows as section 5 describes. It does apply the current-fact predicate.

7. Write Mechanics

Writes are two-phase and idempotent. An interaction is recorded synchronously; distillation runs through the lesson_distillations state machine, which claims the interaction with a token and owner identity, extracts a candidate, and lands in a terminal state with a result_reason. The refusals are the interesting part: not_concrete rejects a platitude before it is stored, exact_duplicate and semantic_duplicate reject a restatement, and rejected_value rejects a pruned value. The refusal is recorded against the interaction.

Outcome crediting is the write that powers everything downstream. When an outcome lands it is attached to the interaction and propagated to the lesson_usage rows for every lesson retrieved, with its source. The store knows not just that a lesson exists but which interactions used it, how they turned out, and who judged.

Facts are written on the hot path by sonder_remember_fact. Validity input that names no entity, decision or provenance goes to the legacy store with an interval; input that does is an authoritative claim and needs the configured source. A superseded statement can be re-asserted later as a new fact, because duplicate detection reads current facts only, and a test asserts it.

Playbook notes are written as proposed with redaction applied first; an exact duplicate of any existing entry body in the topic, whatever its status, is refused, and a near-duplicate is marked. A rejected body therefore cannot be re-proposed verbatim while the rejected entry exists.

Background work is a nightly job (scripts/nightly_self_improve.py): drain distillation, backfill embeddings, prune near-duplicates. The pruner loads the newest 1,024 lessons and compares only those (lesson_pruner.py:26, :29), so a duplicate pair older than that window is never examined. Quarantine and decay are computed at read time and cost one ordered scan of lesson_usage per retrieval.

Operational cost

Distillation is one model call per qualifying interaction — a good outcome, or a failure with a readable error for a pitfall — deferred through the claim state machine, with the nightly job draining what is left. Retrieval embeds the task and scans all lesson vectors in the same space in Python; the prompt carries at most k lessons, a bounded facts block and the approved playbook index, which is frozen per session so it does not move under a provider's prefix cache.

An outbox in the memory schema, one layer from the live path

Every memory database carries an outbox_events table under UNIQUE(aggregate_type, aggregate_id, sequence). Its only writer is OutcomeService.record, which records an outcome and appends an outcome.recorded event in one unit of work. The one production constructor of that service is MemoryFacade.record (sonder_runtime/application/memory/facade.py:63), and nothing calls it; the running system records outcomes through uow.memory.record_outcome in server.py. A reader who queries outbox_events on a working deployment finds it empty.

The event is built with sequence=0 hardcoded (sonder_runtime/application/memory/outcome_service.py:76) and inserted with no conflict clause. An interaction can receive more than one outcome, so the second outcome for the same interaction would raise inside the transaction and roll back the outcome with it, once a caller routes through the service.

8. Agent Integration

The memory adapter is importable, and the runtime drives it from a REPL, a headless service, an HTTP server and an MCP surface. The agent's leverage over lessons is outcome-mediated: it has no save_lesson or forget_lesson tool; it acts, an outcome is recorded, and distillation and quarantine decide what becomes and stays a lesson.

Two memories are written by tool. sonder_remember_fact takes text, a project and optional validity, and sonder_forget_fact deletes by exact id after an exact confirm. playbook_note proposes an entry, and playbook_read returns approved entries for one topic.

Playbook review is the one human gate. The producer is the agent, through playbook_note on MCP or the local agent's tool list. The entry's taint comes from a context variable, not an argument, and the module says why: "they are not public tool arguments that a model could use to self-approve a note." approval_status returns proposed for any tainted note in every approval mode, including auto (sonder_runtime/domain/memory/playbooks.py:215-220).

The resolvers are out of the agent's reach:

  • the sonder playbooks approve CLI and the REPL's /playbooks approve, typed by the operator, which issue and consume a ledger approval bound to the entry's content digest and then set approved;
  • permission_approve, which issues a one-shot approval only for the in-process console object, a developer-role token, or SONDER_ALLOW_PERMISSION_EDITS=1 (server.py:9163-9185), and which the agent loop refuses as a system-operator tool (server.py:18259, sonder_runtime/bootstrap/generic_agent_dispatch.py:30);
  • the playbook directory itself, which the control-plane inventory owns so file tools cannot write an approved entry into it (control_plane_paths.py:358).

human_review is awarded on that store. Two limits are stated rather than counted against it. An agent with a general shell tool could invoke the CLI as a subprocess, as with any CLI-gated review. And an operator who sets SONDER_ALLOW_PERMISSION_EDITS=1 lets a direct MCP client issue the ledger approval that the next /playbooks list reconciles.

Lessons have no review surface; the quarantine that governs them is statistical and automatic. The contribute.py and pull_community.py flow exchanges code modules, not memories.

9. Reliability, Safety, and Trust

Provenance is the strongest work here. Every outcome records who judged it, enforced by schema, and a test guards that no production INSERT INTO outcomes omits the column. The provenance is used in two different ways. Heuristically attributed evidence is dropped from the quarantine evidence and can never ground a distillation. Machine-graded evidence — the code gate running a reply's own code — counts toward quarantine fully, and the test docstring explains the line is "NOT machine-versus-human". So a store fed only machine-graded outcomes is a store grading itself, however carefully the attribution is set.

The quarantine's two statistical guards separate it from the naive version. It asks whether the run is improbable for the lesson's own retrieval band, so a rarely used lesson is not punished for the difficulty of the tasks retrieval sent it, and whether the lesson is individually answerable, so a cohort that always fails together does not let each member count the shared failure. Both are tested with worked probabilities.

The mutation journal is opt-in

With [memory_replication] enabled = true, compose_memory_unit_of_work (sonder_runtime/bootstrap/app.py:187) gives every live unit of work an SQLiteAuthoritativeFactSource for one project scope. Each fact write or delete then appends an ordered memory_replication_log row — source, epoch, sequence, kind, id, version, upsert or delete, payload, digest — in the same SQLite transaction as the fact, and a failed append rolls the fact back. Legacy add_fact and delete_fact refuse the activated project, so the fact cannot be changed around the journal. Nothing in production updates or deletes log rows; prune_before exists and only tests call it. audit_log is awarded on this journal.

It covers facts in one configured project, and the default configuration leaves it off. Lessons, preferences and playbooks have no mutation journal: a lesson is pruned with only a content-free tombstone left, a preference is overwritten in place, and a playbook entry's status is rewritten in its file. refinement_history, with its append-only triggers, would cover preferences and has no production writer.

Residual risks

  • memory_search crosses projects and ignores quarantine (section 5).
  • memory_export dumps lessons, facts, preferences and optionally interactions across projects to the caller; the agent loop refuses it as an operator tool.
  • Quarantine writes no rejected-value record, so a quarantined lesson re-distilled from a fresh interaction can return.
  • The pruner's newest-1,024 window leaves older duplicates unpruned.
  • There is no encryption of the local database; it is as private as the disk.

10. Tests, Evals, and Benchmarks

The suite is large and aimed at the mechanisms this report cares about. tests/test_retriever.py pins the quarantine: test_quarantine_judges_a_lesson_against_its_own_frequency_band (with an explicit p=0.006), test_positive_outcome_rehabilitates_quarantined_lesson, test_quarantine_reaches_probation_and_a_win_is_the_exit and test_quarantine_still_fires_on_an_unambiguously_harmful_lesson. tests/test_learning_health.py:466 and :498 add the shared-blame cases.

The must-not-retrieve cases are the strong kind, each with a returned control in the same populated store. test_prompt_and_trace_omit_quarantined_lesson (tests/test_orchestrator_memory.py:94) asserts a neutral lesson reaches the prompt and trace while the quarantined one reaches neither. test_retrieve_excludes_repeated_unanimous_failure_lesson (tests/test_retriever.py:305) asserts the healthy lesson is returned on both paths and the failing one is not. test_remember_fact_supersedes_and_recall_drops_the_old_fact (tests/test_fact_validity.py:314) asserts the successor is in the injected prompt and in memory_search while the superseded fact is in neither, and :331 asserts an expired fact is stored and not injected. test_a_default_note_is_proposed_and_never_reaches_the_prompt_index (tests/test_playbook_safety_gates.py:57) has no returned control.

The tombstone has tests of its own: tests/test_reflection.py:197 refuses a pruned value by exact text and :216 a reworded one whose embedding matches; tests/test_lesson_pruner.py asserts tombstones carry no text, that a refreshed embedding blocks a planned deletion, and that a large corpus is pruned in the newest window. These are write refusals rather than retrieval omissions.

Nothing asserts that memory_search keeps another project out, and nothing could pass: the function takes no project. An end-to-end retrieval benchmark is also missing. tune_min_sim.py calibrated the 0.62 floor on 22 positive and 15 noise probes, and the band rates are measured from the live store in comments, but no committed run characterises the loop's effect on outcomes.

11. For Your Own Build

Steal

Credit outcomes back to the memories that were retrieved, and record who judged the outcome. The interaction → outcome(source) → lesson_usage chain is the loop most lesson stores omit, and a source column enforced NOT NULL with a closed vocabulary is what lets later math tell a caller's judgement from the machine's.

Before suppressing a memory for bad outcomes, check the base rate and the attribution. A run of losses is evidence only if it is improbable for how often the memory is used, and only the memory's own share of each failure counts.

Make suppression reversible with a sampled probation. A quarantined memory that gets a deterministic fraction of traffic can earn its way back on evidence; a permanently excluded one cannot.

Keep review state out of the agent's arguments. Sonder's playbook taint is a context variable that only the owner's REPL path sets, so no tool argument can mark a note trusted, and the approval is bound to a content digest checked again under the write lock.

Tombstone a rejected value without keeping the value. A normalized-text hash plus the embedding, consulted by exact and semantic match, rejects re-derivation while staying privacy-safe and distinct from erasure.

Give asserted facts a validity interval and an explicit successor. Supersession by id rather than inference, closing the old interval exactly at the new one's start, keeps "what was true" queryable without a resolver.

Avoid

Do not let a search tool bypass the scope the prompt assembler applies. Sonder filters facts and recalls by project when it builds a prompt, and its memory_search returns every project's rows to the same agent.

Do not let outcome provenance be optional. A nullable-with-default source is how a machine-graded outcome becomes indistinguishable from a caller-graded one.

Do not ship an audit table without a writer. refinement_history has triggers, version checks and a rollback path, and preferences change through an upsert that never touches it.

Do not suppress without a rejected-value record if re-derivation is likely. Pruning tombstones and quarantine does not, so the harmful lesson is the one that can come back.

Do not bound a maintenance sweep newest-first without a cursor. The pruner's window never reaches the older half of a large store.

Fit

This suits a builder of a self-improving agent that accumulates procedural lessons and can observe outcomes; the quarantine's statistical guards are worth lifting whole into a different store. It fits a single machine and one local SQLite file, used by one operator.

Walk away if you cannot produce a trustworthy outcome signal, since machine-graded outcomes drive the gate at full weight. Walk away if projects must be isolated from the agent itself, since memory_search reads across them, or if you need a mutation audit on lessons or preferences. Treat the surrounding runtime as out of scope: the memory adapter, the retriever and the playbook store are the parts worth adopting.

12. Open Questions

  • What fraction of outcomes on real traffic carry caller provenance versus machine or unknown? The improvement report shows the buckets separately, and no committed run reports the ratio.
  • Does the same tombstone check ever run against quarantined lessons? At this pin it does not; reason admits only near_duplicate_pruned.
  • Are the quarantine constants — five losses, two distinct tasks, 24-hour probation, one in twenty — tuned against outcomes, or chosen? The band rates are measured; the gates are defended in comments.
  • Is memory_search meant to cross projects on the local surface, or is the missing predicate an omission? The HTTP layer refuses it to accounts as global; nothing on the local surface says why it should be.
  • Will refinement_transactions be routed into the preference writes, and will replication be enabled by default?

A quarantine with a stated null hypothesis rather than a threshold. Evidence is scoped to the epoch since the last grounded success, so a lesson can recover and can also relapse, and probation gives it a production path back to a win.
