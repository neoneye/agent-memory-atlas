On this page

1. Executive Summary

Velantrim Crystal is a local-first evidence and memory kernel in Python whose rule is that finding a fact and trusting it are separate code paths: a truth gate that never admits model output as a world fact decides what enters the canon, and only a VERIFIED, evidenced, unrestricted fact may ground an answer. Its strongest parts are that gate and a grounding predicate that fails closed on any missing field. Its weakest are that erasure and rejection are unjoined records and that the bundled HTTP service trusts whoever may ingest to also approve.

The front page states the rule as "Discovery may propose what deserves inspection. Authority is a separate decision path." The project's documentation leans the same way: an agent-facing router tells a reader not to infer implementation from the narrative README, and a capability matrix lists what is not implemented — a dedicated semantic reader, an ANN index, an NLI filter, a PostgreSQL runtime. The licence is AGPL-3.0, which reaches a network service built on it.

Five mechanisms earn five marks. A fact has a state and a status. It enters as Observed; the truth gate (core/truth_gate.py:23-91) requires a source, refuses a WORLD_FACT whose source_status is LLM_OUTPUT as a fixed invariant no environment variable can lift, and applies a fixed confidence floor of 0.05 to world facts and interpretations while letting subjective claims through as experience. Admission moves it to Validated. Contradicted, Deprecated and Collapsed are terminal. is_strict_canonical (core/canonical_view.py:160-243) lets a fact ground an answer only when its truth_status is exactly VERIFIED and consistent with its claim type and source, its state is Validated or ImmutableCore, and its restricted bit is exactly false.

A curator decides. The review queue lists Observed facts with a diagnosis; approve refuses a conflict until resolve_conflict has been called and overrides a blocked gate only with force, an actor and a reason; reject collapses; each decision is staged under a compare-and-swap on the fact's revision and projected to the canon through an outbox (core/review.py, core/review_decision_store.py). The agent's MCP server is read-only and carries none of these verbs.

A rejected value is remembered. The immune memory (core/immune.py) is a table of claim patterns keyed on their normalized text, each with a type, a severity and the actor who recorded it. ingest, the importer and the review diagnosis screen every claim against it before the guardian and the truth gate, and a claim that contains a recorded pattern whole-token is refused with Immune: in the reason and stays Observed in L1. A curator records a pattern from the CLI, and with strict and learn mode both on, the ingest path records a contradicted claim itself. A force approval passes a blocked claim into Validated stamped CURATOR_OVERRIDE, a status strict grounding refuses, so the override admits the claim and never lets it answer.

Every compliance event is chained. audit_log rows seal seq|ts|event|fact_id|detail|prev_hash in entry_hash, a checkpoint row pins the head so a deleted suffix shows, and an HMAC signs entries when a key is configured (core/audit.py:73-160).

Exclusion is tested on populated reads: a graph walk returns its seed and skips a neighbour whose L1 record is terminal, a search at k=1 skips a restricted exact match for the unrestricted near-duplicate, and a typed review listing omits a restricted pending fact beside an ordinary one.

The boundary of the tombstone mark runs between two records that look alike. Erasure (core/erasure.py:66-201) is physical — L1, graph, evidence, sessions — cascades over DERIVED_FROM edges, refuses Ring Zero, is idempotent, and writes a content-free erasure_log row with a content_hash that no write path reads. That row is a deletion receipt: it proves that something was removed and cannot say what may not return. The immune memory is keyed on the value and read before admission, and it is the one the mark asks for. The two are not joined: erasing a claim does not record its pattern, and recording a pattern does not erase the facts that match it.

Two marks are withheld on definitions. There is no validity interval, and there is no scope key: a restricted bit and a session-wide restriction implement a processing restriction, not a partition.

2. Mental Model

A memory is a fact with a state, and the states are the design. A claim is evidence until the gate admits it; it is canon once admitted; it is an answer only while it is VERIFIED, evidenced and unrestricted.

Truth status is a second axis: VERIFIED, USER_CLAIMED, HYPOTHESIS, SUBJECTIVE, UNVERIFIED, CURATOR_OVERRIDE, assigned by modality and source (core/pipeline.py:872-894). A world fact from an EXTERNAL, DERIVED or OBSERVED source becomes VERIFIED; a user's own report becomes USER_CLAIMED. A fact can be Validated and not VERIFIED, and is_strict_canonical requires both independently rather than inferring one from the other.

Belief ends four ways. A curator rejects a pending fact to Collapsed. A contradiction resolved against a canonical fact moves it to Contradicted, and supersession continues to Deprecated. A restriction withholds a fact from every read without changing its state. Erasure removes it.

The highlighted nodes are the whole argument: the immune screen refuses what has been judged wrong before, the gate cannot be configured to trust the model, and the grounding predicate refuses everything it cannot confirm. Everything downstream exists so that the answer path never has to ask whether something merely looked relevant.

  • truth_gate.py, canonical_view.py, pipeline.py (retrieve, the guardian, generate_answer, the outbox drain), query_pipeline.py (the read-only search and query).
  • review.py, review_decision_store.py, review_projection.py, conflict_decision.py, contradiction.py, contradiction_report.py, curator_auth.py, curator_runtime.py.
  • audit.py, provenance_chain.py, erasure.py, compliance.py.
  • immune.py — the rejected-pattern memory.
  • evidence.py (spans, verification, grounding validity), provenance.py.
  • l3_graph.py — the canon: nodes, vectors, edges, entities, mentions; postgresql_migration*.py written and inactive.
  • reader_*.py — the Reader: source identity, structure, passes, proposition extraction, relations, long context, cross-document links, lexical candidate discovery.
  • api.py (FastAPI), api_ingest_policy.py, cli.py, mcp_server.py, adapters/.

Deployment and ergonomics

  • What has to run: Python and SQLite; the FastAPI service is optional; a Docker image ships. The L3 backend selects LadybugDB when that package is installed and on-disk SQLite otherwise (core/l3_graph.py:1062-1075); Neo4j is selectable. The PostgreSQL path exists as a migration marked inactive and not authorized.
  • Fully local and offline: yes; embeddings are optional, and without an indexed embedder fingerprint the read path falls back to a bounded lexical pass with a reindex_required reason code.
  • API key: every guarded HTTP route returns 401 without VELANTRIM_API_TOKEN unless VELANTRIM_API_ALLOW_UNAUTH_LOCAL=1 is set.
  • Hand-repairable: the store is SQLite with a documented schema, the audit chain is verifiable with a command, and a receipt can be re-verified.
  • Install: pip install -e .; the CI runs a Ring Zero mutation gate and a --cov-fail-under=100 line-coverage gate (.github/workflows/ci.yml).

4. Essential Implementation Paths

Ingest. core/ingest.py:158-372 classifies the utterance (classify_claim), resolves a fact_id from the exact-normalized claim (_resolve_auto_fact, ing: plus 12 hex of an MD5), and returns early on an exact repeat of a Validated fact as an occurrence. It then stores the claim as Observed, screens it against the immune memory, returns accepted: False with an Immune: reason on a match (262-267), and runs the guardian and the truth gate. On a pass it transitions to Validated, sets the truth_status by modality and source, and merges into L3 (311-329); on refusal the fact stays Observed with the reason. In strict mode a contradiction of the canon also blocks, and with learning on the blocked utterance is recorded as a threat by the ingest path itself (292-302). The importer (core/imports.py:78-79) screens the same way; knowledge.ingest_file and write_voluntary (core/volition.py:84) route through ingest.

Immune memory. core/immune.py:85-122: record_threat normalizes the pattern, derives pattern_id from the normalized text, upserts the immune_memory row with threat_type, severity and actor, and appends a content-free immune_threat_recorded audit event; re-recording the same pattern refreshes type and severity and keeps the hit count. forget_threat (124-134) deletes the row and appends immune_threat_forgotten. match_threat (154-175) pads the normalized claim and every recorded pattern with spaces and tests containment, so car never matches scary, and returns the highest-severity match at or above VELANTRIM_IMMUNE_BLOCK_SEVERITY (default 0.5). screen (178-235) returns BLOCK with the matched entry and registers the hit. The CLI exposes immune record, immune forget, immune screen and immune report (core/cli.py:377-388).

The gate. core/truth_gate.py:23-91: no facts is a refusal, and so is a fact without source. A WORLD_FACT with source_status == "LLM_OUTPUT" is a refusal the docstring calls a Ring Zero invariant that "no environment variable, runtime mode or caller option can disable". Subjective claim types pass without an evidentiary bar; world facts and interpretations need confidence >= DEFAULT_MIN_CONFIDENCE, fixed at 0.05 under the version string truth-gate-v1-fixed-0.05. tests/test_truth_gate.py:142-152 sets ENABLE_TRUTH_POLICY to six values and checks the refusal holds.

Read. core/query_pipeline.py:195-243: with no indexed embedder, a bounded lexical pass; otherwise pipeline.retrieve (core/pipeline.py:445-700), a cosine leg over admitted memory and the opt-in demo seed, a lexical leg and a graph walk, fused by reciprocal rank. _l1_terminal_state_blocks and _l1_restricted_blocks (347-374) let a terminal or restricted L1 record win over a stale L3 copy, and _may_propagate_activation and _may_seed_vector_hit (375-440) refuse to walk through such a node.

_resolve_canonical_fact (core/query_pipeline.py:167-192) rebuilds each hit from the L3 node and the L1 row. search_result keeps only facts whose restricted is exactly False (274-277). query drops a VERIFIED fact that has no replayable evidence span (341-356), then hands the rest to generate_answer, whose project_canonical keeps only strict-canonical facts. Every outcome carries a reason code rather than an empty list that looks like a result. HTTP /ask and the CLI ask both call query; pipeline.run, the variant that admits and writes, is called by core/eval.py alone, which the CLI eval command runs.

Review. core/review.py:84-470: pending attaches a diagnosis per item (ready, blocked, conflict), where _diagnose (122-156) runs the immune screen first and then the guardian and the gate. approve (196) applies the rules in section 1, a force approval writing truth_status CURATOR_OVERRIDE and the event review_force_approve. reject (461) moves a fact to Collapsed through transition_esm with a compare-and-swap; resolve_conflict (389) re-runs the diagnosis and refuses a blocked claim; sessions sit at 535-648. review_decision_store.stage_review_decision (165-345) writes the decision, its audit event and the projection intent in one SQLite transaction; a graph backend failure leaves a durable projection_status rather than a lost decision, and drain_projections retries. The projection refuses a participant that has an erasure row (core/review_projection.py:28).

Curator identity. core/curator_runtime.py:49-160 builds one principal from VELANTRIM_CURATOR_ACTOR, _ROLES and _SCOPES, authorizes the action against the role's capabilities and a fact: scope (core/curator_auth.py:117-152), and passes the principal's id to review as the actor. HTTP derives the principal after the same bearer-token check /ingest uses (core/api.py:79-106); unauthenticated local mode substitutes a synthetic ADMIN with scope fact:*.

Audit. core/audit.py:73-160: append_event under BEGIN IMMEDIATE, tail and checkpoint read inside the lock, the entry hash sealed over the previous hash, an optional HMAC; verify_audit_log walks the chain and the checkpoint.

Erase. core/erasure.py:66-201 refuses IMMUTABLE_FACT_IDS, then deletes evidence, import-session entries and the normalized ingest mapping first, so an orphan with nothing else left is erased too. A fact never stored and never erased is a no-op with no receipt, because fabricating an erasure record for data that never existed would be its own Article 30 defect. Otherwise it removes L1, the L3 node and edges, mentions and outbox entries; cascades over DERIVED_FROM with cycle protection when asked; writes the receipt, the audit event and a provenance-chain entry; and returns erased_now=False on a repeat.

Restrict. core/compliance.py:48-60: restrict_processing sets the bit; session-restrict and session-erase (core/cli.py:212-215) apply it or erasure to a whole import session.

5. Memory Data Model

The facts table (core/memory.py:165-178):

Column Role
fact_id stable id from the exact-normalized claim on the auto-id path
claim, source the text and where it came from; both required to ground
confidence 0–1, gated for world facts and interpretations
epistemic_state Observed, Hypothesized, Supported, Validated, Contradicted, Deprecated, Collapsed, ImmutableCore
claim_type WORLD_FACT, interpretation, or a subjective type
source_status UNKNOWN, USER_REPORTED, LLM_OUTPUT, or an independent source
significance a second 0–1, for ranking
restricted the processing-restriction bit, deny-dominant when unknown

truth_status lives on the L3 node and is recombined with the L1 row at read time. A revision column, added by migration, carries the compare-and-swap. store_fact passes claim and metadata through crypto.encrypt, which is the identity unless at-rest encryption is enabled.

The immune_memory table (core/memory.py:199-208): pattern_id (a hash of the normalized pattern, the primary key), pattern as normalized text, threat_type, severity, recorded_at, actor, hits, last_hit_at.

Beside it: evidence_spans binding a fact_id to a byte range and a hash of a source, verified by verify_evidence; erasure_log keyed on fact_id with erased_at, reason, actor, content_hash; audit_log; provenance_chain, a per-fact hash chain written by erasure; chain_checkpoints; review_sessions; import_sessions.

Temporal: created_at and updated_at; no validity interval. bitemporal withheld.

Trust: the state and the status, both read. trust_state earned.

Scoping: none by principal. Curator principals carry fact: scopes, which authorize review writes and filter no read. scope_enforced withheld.

Tombstone: the immune_memory row, keyed on the normalized value and read by ingest, imports and the review diagnosis before the gate. The erasure record is not it: its content_hash is read by no write path. Its fact_id is read by the review projection, which refuses to project a decision about an erased id; on the auto-id path that id is derived from the claim, but ingest reads no erasure row, so a re-ingested erased claim the gate passes is admitted. tombstone earned on the immune memory, with its limits in section 9.

6. Retrieval Mechanics

Two surfaces share one retriever. pipeline.retrieve is the working recall: a cosine leg over embeddings, a lexical leg, and a graph walk that spreads activation through neighbours, decays per hop and never passes through a terminal or restricted node or along a truth-maintenance edge, with L1 winning over a stale L3 copy. It fetches three times k from the vector index so that restricted rows cannot starve the top-k. query_pipeline.search and query are the read-only public surface: they resolve hits to canonical facts, drop anything not restricted == False, and never write. A store with no indexed embedder answers from a bounded lexical pass and says so in the result.

Grounding is stricter than retrieval. An answer may cite a fact only when is_strict_canonical holds and, for a VERIFIED fact, has_valid_evidence_for_grounding finds a replayable span; the documentation's own line is same fact_id != same claim, and a VERIFIED fact whose evidence has gone stale loses current standing without losing its history.

The MCP search tool returns non-canonical facts with their truth_status and epistemic_state attached, so the agent can see a USER_CLAIMED fact and its label; get_fact returns any unrestricted fact by id in any state.

Failure modes: the lexical pass is deterministic and bounded by design, and the semantic reader is declared not implemented, so recall on paraphrase depends on the optional embeddings; a store with many terminal facts pays the L1 lookup per hit.

7. Write Mechanics

Gated, not extracted. A claim is classified by regex and stored as typed by the caller; nothing summarises. consolidate() decays confidence and has no caller outside its own module. The immune screen and the gate are the write mechanic. Crystal lets a feeling in as a feeling without letting it become a world fact, and refuses to let its own output be promoted by any switch: write_voluntary, the system's self-authored write, goes through ingest pinned to USER_REPORTED, so a world fact it writes is USER_CLAIMED at best.

Source status is declared. The gate trusts source_status. On HTTP an unprivileged caller may send only USER_REPORTED or LLM_OUTPUT, and EXTERNAL, DERIVED or OBSERVED need import mode, evidence references and VELANTRIM_API_PRIVILEGED_INGEST=1 (core/api_ingest_policy.py); on the Python path the label is whatever the caller passes.

Conflicts. core/contradiction.py classifies a pair of claims by polarity, numbers and content overlap; a conflict diagnosis blocks approve until resolve_conflict records a decision with metadata on the losing candidate (conflict_decision.py).

Concurrency. Every decision is a compare-and-swap on the fact's revision; a CAS miss aborts without touching L3 (test_approve_aborts_on_cas_miss_without_l3_merge). A secondary L3 sync — restriction, reconciliation — merges a Validated fact only when its graph node exists, with an explicit exception for outbox recovery and supersession (core/memory.py:60-88).

Malicious input: a claim is text; the gate checks provenance and confidence, not content. Ring Zero facts cannot be erased or overwritten.

Operational cost

  • A write is one SQLite transaction plus a graph merge; the outbox absorbs a graph failure.
  • A fact is retrievable immediately by the lexical leg; the vector leg after reindex_embeddings.
  • No background pass rewrites the store.
  • The answer path returns a receipt with the grounding facts and a verification route (/receipt, /verify-receipt).

8. Agent Integration

The agent-facing surface is a deliberately read-only MCP server. core/mcp_server.py (279 lines, stdio JSON-RPC) exposes search, memory_report, get_fact, fact_history, find_conflicts and verify_receipt; none calls a writer, so an agent over MCP can read canon, inspect a fact's lifecycle, classify a claim against the store and verify a receipt, and cannot ingest or approve (tests/test_mcp_server.py, 20 cases).

The HTTP service is the write and curation surface. A client calls /ingest and /ask, receives a receipt it can re-verify, and reads /evidence/{fact_id}; a curator works /review/queue, /review/approve, /review/reject and the conflict routes, plus the CLI immune commands. The service maps one bearer token to one configured principal, in the words of docs/security/CURATOR_WRITE_AUTH.md "one bearer token mapped to one curator principal". A deployment that gives an agent the token for /ingest therefore gives it /review/approve too; separating the two means running the review routes behind a different host or authentication dependency, which the project's ADR-018 names as host work.

core/curator_runtime.py is not a machine curator: it is the bundled write bridge from an authenticated principal to core.review, deriving the audit actor from the principal and checking its role, capabilities and candidate and target scopes before any mutation. The agent-facing documentation (docs/ai/README.md) is a router with a source-of-truth order that ranks live GitHub, tests and CI above the prose, and a lifecycle overlay it tells a reader not to trust past its date.

The pipeline and the truth gate each carry a comment reading "Known limitation[s] (not scheduled — Crystal V1 is freeze/stability with no automatic next milestone)", naming a full ESM transition matrix and Laplace confidence among them. A gap stated as unscheduled is easier to evaluate than one stated as next.

9. Reliability, Safety, and Trust

What holds. The gate's one invariant is not configurable; canonical grounding fails closed on a missing, malformed or inconsistent field; a restricted bit is deny-dominant and wins over a stale graph. Decisions are atomic with their audit event; the audit chain has a checkpoint and nothing in production rewrites or prunes it; erasure is idempotent, cascades over derived facts and refuses Ring Zero. A recorded pattern blocks on sight across ingest, import and review, and recording or forgetting one is audited (tests/test_immune.py). A force approval needs the ADMIN role on HTTP, an actor and a reason, is audited, and yields CURATOR_OVERRIDE, which cannot ground a strict answer (tests/test_force_override_audit.py:147-154).

Where the tombstone stops. The immune memory is empty until someone writes to it, and erasure does not: an erased claim is remembered as a hash in erasure_log and as nothing in immune_memory, so a claim erased for being wrong returns unless a curator also recorded its pattern. In the other direction, erasing a fact leaves any recorded pattern that quotes it in the immune table as normalized text, which crypto.encrypt never touches. immune forget removes a pattern. Containment is whole-token, so a paraphrase of a recorded pattern is not caught. A blocked claim is kept as an Observed row in L1 rather than refused at the store. reconcile.supersede admits through the gate without the immune screen; it has no caller in core/.

Where the review boundary stops. The MCP server holds no verb. The HTTP service holds them behind the same credential as ingest, and in unauthenticated local mode behind none, every caller acting as the synthetic api-curator admin. The Python review.approve takes actor as a string.

What is withheld, and why. bitemporal: no validity interval. scope_enforced: restriction is per fact and per session, and the curator's fact: scopes authorize writes, not reads.

What is convention. The audit detail is content-free by contract, not by schema. approve writes the curator's free-text note, and a force approval its reason, into it (core/review.py:284-298), so a curator who quotes a claim has written personal data into an append-only table. The Reader's authorization flags in the manifest are documentation, enforced by the project's own review process.

11. For Your Own Build

Steal

  • Make the model-output rule an invariant, not a setting. One if that no configuration reaches is worth more than a policy file.
  • Ground on a pure predicate that fails closed. is_strict_canonical never infers verification from confidence or state, cross-checks a VERIFIED label against the policy that could have produced it, and rejects any missing field; it is testable in isolation and is tested thirty-nine ways.
  • Give an override its own status. A force approval in Crystal admits the claim as CURATOR_OVERRIDE, which the grounding predicate refuses, so an override is visible and never silently becomes evidence.
  • Stage the decision with its audit event and its projection intent in one transaction, and let the graph fail separately with a durable status.
  • Checkpoint the audit chain head. A hash chain alone cannot see a truncated tail.
  • Cascade erasure over derivation edges. Derived personal data should not outlive its source.
  • Screen against the rejected patterns before the gate, on every admitting path. Crystal calls the same screen from ingest, import and review.

Avoid

  • An erasure that does not tell the rejection memory. Crystal keeps both records and joins neither to the other; if a claim is erased because it was wrong, record its pattern too, or say that it may return.
  • One credential for writing and approving. The bundled HTTP mapping makes the ingest token the curator token; give the reviewer a credential the writer does not hold.
  • Treating a restriction bit as a scope. It withholds one fact from everyone; it partitions nothing.

Fit

Crystal fits a team whose memory must answer where did this come from and may we say it before what is relevant — compliance-shaped assistants, research memory with sources, anything where a wrong confident answer costs more than a refusal. It is not a conversational memory and does not claim to be: there is no conversational extractor and no scope key; the Reader has source and session foundations and a proposition extractor, and a dedicated autonomous or semantic reader is declared not implemented.

12. Open Questions

  • Will erasure feed the immune memory, or clear it? An erasure with a reason of wrong carries the normalized claim that record_threat would need, and an erasure for privacy would want any pattern quoting the claim gone; the two tables have no edge between them.
  • Does a force approval of an immune-blocked claim reach the pattern's hit count or its actor? The override is audited on the fact; whether the pattern learns that it was overridden is not visible in the tests.
  • When the PostgreSQL runtime is authorized, does row-level restriction follow the restricted bit or stay in application code?
