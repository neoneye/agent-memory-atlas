# scan cannot tell a helper repository from prior art, a benchmark or a rival, so
# nothing goes in here automatically.

# One `owner/repo` per line. Everything after `#` is a comment, and the reason a
# line is here belongs in it — a bare entry is one nobody can re-evaluate later.
#
# Forks land as `<owner>--<repo>` in the archive organisation, same as the rest.

# repository is never archived by that path: if it disappears, whatever its
